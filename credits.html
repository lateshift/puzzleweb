Playback

ANSI Second Reality is the terminal backend for SecondReality++. SecondReality++ is by XorJS and is released under the Unlicense. It is based on SecondRealityW32 by Gargaj / Conspiracy.

Browser playback also uses SDL3 and Emscripten.
